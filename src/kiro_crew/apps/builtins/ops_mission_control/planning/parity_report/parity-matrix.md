# Parity matrix — every source capability, by domain

Companion to [README.md](README.md). This file is the complete per-domain matrix: every
capability from the four research passes, with its verdict, evidence, and for gaps the
operator impact and placement. The README ranks *across* domains by cost; this file is
organised by domain for lookup.

Paths in the source tree are given relative to its package root and prefixed `source:`
(or `source-skillset:` for the second package). App paths are
relative to `src/kiro_crew/apps/builtins/ops_mission_control/` unless prefixed
`src/kiro_crew/`, `website/`, `docs/` or `src/kiro_crew/builtin_skills/`.

Verdicts: **Implemented** (present, and where noted stronger) · **Partial** (present but
materially incomplete) · **Missing** · **Deliberate** (declined with a recorded reason).

| Domain | Impl | Partial | Missing | Deliberate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1. Signal ingestion, claiming, dispatch, investigation | 20 | 12 | 6 | 7 |
| 2. Knowledge, memory, learning, the ledger | 10 | 7 | 4 | 6 |
| 3. Rotation, team, handover, multi-instance | 11 | 8 | 7 | 12 |
| 4. Proactive automation, reporting, digests | 10 | 7 | 9 | 14 |
| **Total** | **51** | **34** | **26** | **39** |
